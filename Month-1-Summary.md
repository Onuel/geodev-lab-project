# Month 1 Summary

## Research Question
To identify settlements in Eti-Osa Local Government Area that are located on low-lying land and within 200 metres of a watercourse?

## Operation Run and Why
- I used Buffer, Intersection and DEM-based elevation statistics 
- This was to examine settlement proximity to waterways, and elevation characteristics.

## Expected vs. Obtained
- I expected to identify settlements within 200 m of waterways and compare their elevation
- I expected to identify settlements that are Low-Lying under 5m in tanderm with the elevation.
- I obtained 1921 of 5903 settlements within 200 m of waterways alongside settlement-level elevation information.
- I obtained 1344 of 5903 settlements that are Low-Lying under 5m


## What Surprised Me
- Most of the settlements are Low-Lying and very few settlements were not


## Data Still Needed
- Future updates will incorporate population, rainfall, drainage, flood-event, and other relevant data for more comprehensive flood modelling.
